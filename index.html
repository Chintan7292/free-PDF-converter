How it works & is it safe?

Every tool runs entirely inside your own browser. Your documents are never uploaded — which is exactly why it's private, free, and works offline once loaded.

Private by design

Files are read and processed on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, so nobody — including us — can see them.

No sign-up, no limits

No account, no email, no watermarks on your output. Open the page and start — as many files as you like.

Works offline

After the page loads once, the tools keep working with no internet connection. Your data stays with you.

Note: the tools in the “Needs a server” section are the only ones that would require uploading — they aren't available here, precisely to keep this promise.
